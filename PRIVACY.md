# myClipper Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

myClipper is a desktop app by Lum Wen-Shen that turns long videos into short captioned clips and uploads them to the user's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts.

## What the app accesses

When you connect a YouTube account, myClipper uses the YouTube Data API with the `youtube.upload` scope to upload videos you have chosen, with the title, description and publish time you set. It does not read your watch history, subscriptions, comments, analytics or any other channel data.

## Where your data is kept

Sign-in tokens and settings are stored only on your own computer, in your user profile folder. They are never sent to the developer or any server other than Google's (and TikTok's or Meta's when you connect those accounts). myClipper has no server, no analytics and no advertising, and it does not sell or share any data.

## Revoking access

Disconnect myClipper at any time from your Google account's security settings (https://myaccount.google.com/permissions) or by deleting the app's settings folder. Removing access stops all uploads.

## Google API Services

myClipper's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube uploads are subject to the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
